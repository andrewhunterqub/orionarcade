Privacy

Orion Arcade is run by Orion Design. You can play every game without an account. This page explains what happens to your data, with or without one.

Playing without an account

Game progress and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, Facebook, Microsoft, GitHub or an emailed link, we receive:

  • Account details: a unique account ID, your name, email address and profile picture, as shared by the sign-in provider you choose. We use these only to sign you in and show who's signed in.
  • Game progress: each game's save data, so you can continue on any device.

We don't post anything to your accounts, read your contacts, show ads, or sell or share your data.

Sign-in and saved progress are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data on our behalf. Only you can read or change your saved progress.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all progress saved to it, sign in, open the account menu at the top right of the page, and choose Delete account. Everything is removed straight away.

If you can't sign in, contact us through oriondesign.xyz and we'll delete it for you.

To remove progress stored in your browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page.
